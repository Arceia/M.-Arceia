Prologue

The Door at the End of the Field

There are stories about places that do not exist. Most people hear those stories once, laugh about them, and continue with their lives. Miru asks for directions.

That was how she ended up beyond the last farm, halfway through a field of grass tall enough to slap her in the face, following instructions from an elderly shepherd who had ended his tale with the words, “But do not actually go there.”

And continue like this.
